What We Believe

We believe that salvation is entirely by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by human performance, guilt, or religious striving. Grace doesn't wait for you to be "perfect" before it equips you—the moment you accepted Christ, He gave you everything that pertains to life and godliness (2 Peter 1:3). You aren't trying to earn His lifestyle; you're learning to live out of the divine life He already placed inside you.
The Bible is God's inspired, reliable revelation for human life. We interpret the Scriptures through the lens of Jesus Christ and His new covenant of love, truth, and reconciliation.
The Holy Spirit indwells every believer as Comforter, Teacher, and Guide. He imparts spiritual gifts, produces Christ-like character, and empowers the Church for supernatural witness and signs.
We are a community of authentic believers who take off the religious masks. We believe every disciple is called to reach someone with the love of Christ. Through our signature #EachOneReachOne mission, we take the transforming Gospel into our neighborhoods, workplaces, and families across Port Harcourt and the world.
